# v0.7 — Under the Glass

First milestone: a second camera mode over the same simulation state.

- Double-click an active settlement to descend into a 2.5D close view.
- Press Esc or RETURN TO WORLD to exit.
- Visible settlement details derive from live population, culture, dynasties, founder, monument, stores, and day/night state.
- The close view is a rendering layer, not a disconnected second simulation.
